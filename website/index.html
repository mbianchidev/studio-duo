Why Studio Duo exists

An entire record.
A tool that's yours.

For the solo musician making a record at home. For the producer tracking a whole band. Studio Duo's mission is to make the full production path possible in one open, dependable DAW.

Modern metal gets first-class tools: linked performance edits, drum programming, DI and reamp workflows, and dense routing. The fundamentals stay familiar, whatever you play.

You make the decisions.

No AI generation, assistants, or cloud models. Deterministic analysis and editing tools help you work; they don't decide what your record should be.

Your session stays yours.

No cloud service or required account. Documented project files, portable copies, and open interchange keep your work movable.

Keep the performance.

Non-destructive editing, comping, and tone workflows preserve the source. Try a different direction without throwing away the take.

Open, from the start.

An AGPLv3 open-source core. Modern plug-in formats. DAWproject exchange. A studio you can inspect, contribute to, and build on.

Tracking & editing

Track together.
Edit together.

Record sample-aligned multitrack audio. Build the performance with take lanes and comping. Link edits across drum mics, doubled guitars, or DI and amp tracks without losing their relative timing.

  • Punch, loop recording, and count-in
  • Tempo maps and a routable metronome
  • Transient tools, elastic audio, and crossfades
  • CoreAudio on macOS; ASIO-compatible interfaces on Windows

Tested with a Focusrite Scarlett Duo on macOS ARM (Apple silicon), using CoreAudio. Windows ASIO support is best effort only.

MIDI & metal drums

Drums you can see.
Patterns you can feel.

Play and record native MIDI drums with the on-screen kit of twelve performance pads visible as you perform. Click the pads or use your keyboard, then refine each recorded hit in the drum grid or piano roll.

Use the built-in drum instrument, genre sound presets, kit tuning, blast-beat and double-kick tools, and seeded humanization. The song transport, metronome, and count-in stay part of the recording workflow. Switch to geometry to see connected rhythm rings without changing the notes.

Mixing & plug-ins

Make room for
every signal.

Shape a dense mix with sends, sidechains, auxes, nested buses, and sample-accurate automation. Build nested plug-in racks and parallel chains with latency compensation that keeps the branches aligned.

  • VST3, CLAP, and Audio Units on macOS
  • Sandboxed standard plug-in processing
  • Native parametric EQ, compressor, and true-peak limiter
  • Built-in gate, reverb, delay, tuner, and signal generator
  • Gain/polarity tools, drum instrument, and guitar/bass amps

ARA 2 uses an explicit compatibility mode.

Universal macOS DMG / Windows x64 installer & portable ZIP / Checksums included

Windows support is best effort only. This is a one-person development team without a Windows testing device, and limited development time restricts hands-on Windows testing.

Desktop system requirements

macOS

  • macOS 12 Monterey or later
  • Apple silicon (ARM64) or Intel (x86_64); universal build
  • CoreAudio-compatible interface or built-in audio

Hands-on audio-interface testing: Focusrite Scarlett Duo on macOS ARM.

Windows / best effort

  • Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit target platforms
  • x64-compatible system
  • Microsoft Visual C++ x64 Runtime; included by the installer
  • ASIO-compatible interface and driver, or Windows Audio fallback

CI builds and checks do not replace physical Windows audio-device testing.

Linux is unsupported. Audio-driver integrations and the differences between distributions are beyond the available time of the one-person development team.

RAM, storage, and processing needs depend on your project media, track count, and plug-ins; fixed performance minima have not been validated.
